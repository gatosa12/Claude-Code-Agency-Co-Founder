## Overview

An installation ad does two jobs at once: it **sells** the outcome and **shows** how fast the viewer can get it. The viewer should finish the video thinking "that was easy -- I can do that in two minutes." Every frame either removes a reason not to install or shows the payoff of installing.

This template gives you a 60-second master video built from **4 production segments**, a full voiceover script, on-screen text, a shot list, brand style rules, cutdowns, and bonus prompts for generating variations and troubleshooting clips with AI video tools.

Replace every `{{placeholder}}` before production. Steps marked `[CUSTOMIZE]` change from app to app.

## Brand-Aligned Visual Style

**Look:** Clean, bright, high-contrast. Lots of negative space. The product UI is the hero -- nothing competes with it.

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Screen captures | Record at 4K / 60fps, scale to 1080p for crisp zooms. Hide personal data, notifications, bookmarks and clutter. Use a fresh demo account with realistic sample data. |
| Cursor | Enlarged cursor (1.5x) with a soft click ripple in {{brand_accent}}. Smooth, deliberate movement -- never jittery. |
| Zooms | Zoom 120-180% into each click target, ease in/out over 0.4s. Never zoom and pan at the same time. |
| Device frames | Place screen recordings inside a clean device mockup (laptop, phone) on a {{brand_neutral}} or soft gradient background. |
| Live-action | Shallow depth of field, soft key light, neutral modern workspace. Real hands on real devices. |
| Text | {{font_headline}} for headlines, max 6 words per line, max 2 lines on screen. Safe margins: 10% on all sides (15% bottom in 9:16 for platform UI). |
| Step badges | Numbered circle badges ("1", "2", "3") in {{brand_primary}} in the top-left of each step. |
| Motion | Subtle -- slide + fade 8-12px, 0.3s. Avoid spins, bounces and stock transitions. |
| Music | Modern, light electronic or acoustic, 100-120 BPM, ducked to -18 LUFS under voiceover. |
| Captions | Always burned in for 9:16 and 1:1 (most viewers watch muted). |
| Logo | Logo sting at open (max 1s) and end card. Watermark bottom-right at 60% opacity optional. |

## The 4 Video Segments (60s Master)

| # | Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---|---------|------|---------|
| 1 | Hook + Problem | 0:00-0:08 | Stop the scroll, name the pain |
| 2 | Download + Install | 0:08-0:25 | Prove installation is fast and simple |
| 3 | Setup + Configure | 0:25-0:45 | Walk through the key setup steps to first value |
| 4 | In Action + CTA | 0:45-0:60 | Show the payoff, add proof, drive the click |

### Segment 1 -- Hook + Problem (0:00-0:08)

**Goal:** Earn the next 5 seconds. Open on the outcome or the pain, never on a logo.

| Shot | Visual | On-Screen Text | Voiceover |
|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| 1A (0:00-0:03) | Close-up: frustrated {{target_viewer}} at a desk, {{pain_point}} visible (missed alerts, messy spreadsheet, ringing phone). | "Still {{pain_point_short}}?" | "Still losing time to {{pain_point}}?" |
| 1B (0:03-0:06) | Quick snap-cut to the {{app_name}} dashboard showing the finished result ({{aha_moment}}). | "Fix it in {{time_to_value}}." | "{{app_name}} fixes that -- and you can set it up in {{time_to_value}}." |
| 1C (0:06-0:08) | Logo sting on {{brand_primary}} background, app icon pops in. | {{app_name}} logo + "Setup guide" | "Here's how." |

**Hook alternatives** (A/B test the first 3 seconds):
- Result-first: "This took me 2 minutes to set up. Watch."
- Question: "What if {{pain_point}} just... stopped?"
- Stat: "{{proof_point}} -- here's how to join them in 3 steps."

### Segment 2 -- Download + Install (0:08-0:25)

**Goal:** Remove "installing software is a hassle" as an objection. Show every click, cut every wait.

| Shot | Visual | On-Screen Text | Voiceover |
|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| 2A (0:08-0:12) | Screen capture in laptop mockup: browser opens {{cta_url}}, cursor clicks "Download" / "Get Started". Zoom on button. | Badge **1** "Go to {{cta_url_short}}" | "Step one: head to {{cta_url_short}} and click Download." |
| 2B (0:12-0:17) | `[CUSTOMIZE]` Installer runs. Show the install path for each platform as a split-screen: Windows installer / macOS drag-to-Applications / App Store "Get" button. Progress bar time-ramped (speed up 4-8x). | "Windows · Mac · iOS · Android" | "It works on {{platforms}} -- the installer takes care of everything." |
| 2C (0:17-0:21) | Permission prompt appears (e.g., allow notifications, microphone, or admin rights). Cursor clicks "Allow". Small callout explains why. | "Allow access → so it can {{permission_reason}}" | "When asked, allow access so {{app_name}} can {{permission_reason}}." |
| 2D (0:21-0:25) | App launches to a clean welcome screen. Subtle confetti or checkmark animation in {{brand_accent}}. | ✓ "Installed" | "And you're in." |

**Production notes:**
- Never show a real progress bar in real time -- ramp it or jump-cut.
- If the install requires a reboot or a long download, cut it and add a "(~1 min)" timestamp label instead.
- For web apps, replace 2B with "Sign up with Google / email" and 2C with email verification.

### Segment 3 -- Setup + Configure (0:25-0:45)

**Goal:** Walk through the 2-3 setup steps that get to first value. Clarity over completeness -- link to full docs for the rest.

| Shot | Visual | On-Screen Text | Voiceover |
|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| 3A (0:25-0:31) | `[CUSTOMIZE]` Sign-in / account creation. Cursor fills fields (pre-typed, fast), clicks "Continue". Zoom on SSO button. | Badge **2** "Sign in or create an account" | "Step two: sign in -- or create a free account in seconds." |
| 3B (0:31-0:38) | `[CUSTOMIZE]` The core setup action: connect an integration, import data, pick a template, or set preferences. Highlight the one critical toggle or field with a glowing outline. | Badge **3** "{{setup_step_3}}" | "Step three: {{setup_step_3_vo}}. Just pick what fits and click Save." |
| 3C (0:38-0:45) | Side-by-side: settings panel on the left, live preview updating on the right as settings change. | "Customize it your way" | "Tweak {{customization_options}} anytime -- changes apply instantly." |

**Production notes:**
- Pre-fill or speed-type form entries so typing never takes more than 1s on screen.
- Use realistic demo data that matches the {{target_viewer}} (e.g., a dental clinic's calendar, not "Test User 1").
- If there are more than 3 setup steps, group them ("Connect your tools") and show a 3-step progress indicator.

### Segment 4 -- In Action + CTA (0:45-0:60)

**Goal:** Show the payoff, prove it works for people like the viewer, and make the next step obvious.

| Shot | Visual | On-Screen Text | Voiceover |
|------|--------|----------------|-----------|
| 4A (0:45-0:51) | `[CUSTOMIZE]` The "aha" moment in real use: {{aha_moment}}. Cut to live-action of the {{target_viewer}} relaxed, smiling, getting on with their day. | "{{benefit_headline}}" | "Now {{app_name}} {{core_benefit}} -- automatically." |
| 4B (0:51-0:55) | Proof: star rating, logo bar, or a 1-line testimonial card. | "{{proof_point}}" | "Join {{proof_point}}." |
| 4C (0:55-1:00) | End card on {{brand_primary}}: app icon, {{offer}}, {{cta_url}}, platform badges (App Store / Google Play / Download for Windows / Mac). Button pulses once. | "{{cta}}" + "{{offer}}" + {{cta_url}} | "{{cta}} at {{cta_url_short}} -- {{offer}}." |

## Full Voiceover Script (60s, ~150 words)

> Still losing time to {{pain_point}}?
> {{app_name}} fixes that -- and you can set it up in {{time_to_value}}. Here's how.
>
> **Step one:** head to {{cta_url_short}} and click Download. It works on {{platforms}} -- the installer takes care of everything. When asked, allow access so {{app_name}} can {{permission_reason}}. And you're in.
>
> **Step two:** sign in -- or create a free account in seconds.
>
> **Step three:** {{setup_step_3_vo}}. Just pick what fits and click Save. Tweak {{customization_options}} anytime -- changes apply instantly.
>
> Now {{app_name}} {{core_benefit}} -- automatically. Join {{proof_point}}.
>
> {{cta}} at {{cta_url_short}} -- {{offer}}.

**Voice direction:** Warm, confident, unhurried -- like a helpful colleague, not an announcer. ~150 words per minute. Slight smile in the voice on the CTA. Pause 0.3s before each "Step".

**AI voice option:** ElevenLabs or similar; pick a clear, mid-range voice, stability ~50%, style ~20%. Generate each segment separately so you can retime without regenerating the whole script.

|----------|-------|---------------------|
