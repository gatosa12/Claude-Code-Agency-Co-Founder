## Compliance flags (page + customer videos)

| Source | Issue | Safe alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Video: "Boosted my fertility and prenatal health" | Health outcome attributed to product | "Zinc contributes to normal fertility and reproduction" |
| Video: "…for energy and hormone balance" | "Hormone balance" not authorised | "B12 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism" / "Iodine contributes to normal thyroid function" |
| Video: "Game changer for post-menopausal women" | Personal-attribute targeting (Meta) | Organic only, or use the clip with a different caption |
| Page sections: pregnancy, skin conditions, sexual health | Implies treating conditions; FDA disclaimer does not cover EU | Use authorised "contributes to normal…" wording only |
| Capsule Quantity Disclaimer | Count varies | Say "one capsule each morning", never a count |
