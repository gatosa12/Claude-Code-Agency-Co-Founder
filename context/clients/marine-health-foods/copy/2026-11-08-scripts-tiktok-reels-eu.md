# OysterMax — Short-Form Video Scripts (EU)

Rules for all scripts: hook lands in the first 1–2 seconds · on-screen text carries the message with sound off · one CTA · health wording only as "contributes to normal…" · never state a capsule count.

## Script 3 — Social Proof / UGC: "Why it's in my routine" (≈30s)

Use a real customer from the existing video set **with their permission and a paid-partnership label if they were compensated**. Brief them with this structure — they speak in their own words, but must not claim the product treated or caused a health result.

| Time | Beat | Prompt for the creator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Hook (holding bottle to camera) | "The one supplement I never run out of." |
| 2–8s | Why they looked for it | Their real reason, framed as a habit ("I wanted something from real food, not a lab"). No symptoms or conditions. |

| 18–25s | Authorised benefit | Read as written: "It's high in zinc, which contributes to normal immune function." |
| 25–30s | CTA | "They do 10% off if you get three. Link's below." |
